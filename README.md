# IBM-HR-Dashboard
# 📊 Executive HR Attrition & Retention Analytics

An end-to-end People Analytics solution designed to diagnose organizational turnover, isolate critical attrition drivers, and deliver actionable strategic retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Employee turnover represents a significant hidden cost in modern enterprises—impacting productivity, team morale, and recruitment expenditures. This project provides an interactive **HR Attrition Dashboard** analyzing workforce trends across **1,470 employees**. 

Key analytics reveal an overall **attrition rate of 16.12%** (237 departures), with heightened turnover concentrated in specific departments (Sales & HR), operational roles (Sales Representatives & Lab Technicians), and key tenure windows (1–3 years).

---

## 💡 Key Dashboard Insights

### 1. High-Level Metrics
* **Total Headcount:** `1,470`
* **Attrition Count:** `237`
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** `16.12%`
* **Average Employee Age:** `37 years`

### 2. Department & Job Role Vulnerabilities
* **Sales Department:** Highest departmental attrition rate at **20.63%**.
* **Human Resources:** Second highest departmental churn at **19.05%**.
* **Sales Representatives:** Standout high-risk role with a massive **39.76% attrition rate**.
* **Lab Technicians & HR Staff:** Also exhibit elevated attrition rates at **23.94%** and **23.08%** respectively.
* **Leadership Roles:** Managers (`4.90%`) and Research Directors (`2.50%`) demonstrate strong workforce stability.

### 3. Primary Turnover Drivers
* **OverTime Workload:** A disproportionately high percentage of employees working regular overtime leave the company compared to non-overtime staff.
* **Tenure Vulnerability (1–3 Years):** Attrition peaks dramatically within the first 3 years of service before dropping off substantially after 10 years.
* **Business Travel Frequency:** Higher frequency of business travel strongly correlates with increased turnover likelihood.
